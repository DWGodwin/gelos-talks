# Tool logos

Logos shown under the pipeline stages and under the app's dashboard
(`tools:` in `_pipeline/labels.yml`). SVGs were passed through a small
cleaner that drops scripts, event attributes, external hrefs and editor
namespaces; nothing else was changed. PNGs were only resized.
